    • Lamborghini Urus

    The Lamborghini Urus is a high-performance luxury "Super SUV" that blends sports car handling with SUV versatility, featuring a powerful V8 engine and an optional plug-in hybrid powertrain (Urus SE) for enhanced performance and efficiency. With a bold, angular design, an upscale interior filled with advanced technology, and customizable drive modes, the Urus offers a unique combination of luxury, everyday usability, and exceptional speed, capable of handling various road conditions and terrains.

    Engine

    Lamborghinis engine sends data to the internet through the vehicle's onboard computer and telematics systems. This is part of the "Lamborghini Connect" suite of services, which collects diagnostic and performance information from the engine's Engine Control Unit (ECU) and other sensors.

    Technology

    A Lamborghini Urus connects to the internet via its built-in Lamborghini Infotainment System (LIS), which uses a cellular data connection to provide a 4G WiFi hotspot and web-powered information for connected services like the Lamborghini Connect suite and the UNICA app. This allows for features such as connected navigation, remote control of vehicle functions through the smartphone app, and integration with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto for access to smartphone apps and digital assistants

    Multi-Connection Concept

    Lamborghini's multi-connection concept connects to the internet through a car's internal cellular data connection and a smartphone app, the Lamborghini UNICA app, which uses a cellular network to access the internet. This connection allows for real-time access to web-powered information via the in-car infotainment system and remote management of vehicle features, like climate control, navigation, and security

    Lamborghini Connect

    Lamborghini Connect uses a car's internal built-in modem and a car data SIM card to connect to the internet via a cellular data network, similar to how a Wi-Fi router connects to the internet through a broadband connection. This connection allows the vehicle to function as a Wi-Fi hotspot for up to five devices and enables services such as over-the-air software updates, vehicle tracking, and remote management through the Lamborghini UNICA app.
